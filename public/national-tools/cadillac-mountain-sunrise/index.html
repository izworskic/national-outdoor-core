The decision

What this tool is trying to answer

A sunrise trip has three gates: the sun has to rise at a known time, the sky has to give you a chance to see it, and you need legal access to the summit road during the reservation season. This tool keeps those decisions together.

  • The sunrise time is calculated for Cadillac Mountain, not copied from a generic city forecast.
  • Cloud wording near sunrise is summarized from the NWS hourly forecast.
  • During the in-season reservation period, check the official Acadia reservation page before driving up.

Source-first

See the evidence behind the answer

Sunrise is calculated from the mountain coordinates. NWS hourly forecast periods near sunrise provide the cloud/weather signal. NPS controls summit-road reservations.

How to use this result

Make the decision from the status, freshness and evidence together

Start with the current decision and the trip consequence above it, then check the timestamp and source excerpt. A favorable condition is not a blanket all-clear. If the tool returns VERIFY or LIVE SOURCE UNAVAILABLE, open the official source instead of treating an old reading as current. Recheck close to departure because roads, alerts, water levels and mountain weather can change after you first plan the trip.
