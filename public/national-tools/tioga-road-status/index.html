The decision

Why an open Tioga Road is not the entire answer

Think of the journey as three connected pieces: the western Highway 120 approach, the road through Yosemite's granite high country, and the eastern Highway 120 approach above Lee Vining. NPS governs the park segment. Caltrans covers state highways beyond it. The route workbench shows exactly what has been checked and what still needs direct verification.

  • Winter storms can shut the high country quickly, sometimes after favorable conditions earlier in the day.
  • The park may publish construction delays or overnight parking restrictions even when Tioga Road is open.
  • Do not infer that Glacier Point Road, Yosemite Valley services or the California approaches are open just because Tioga is.

Source-first

Read the NPS road note, not just its badge

NPS Yosemite current conditions is the authority for the park road; Caltrans reports Highway 120 conditions outside the park. The tool isolates Tioga's own row, captures its notes and reports publication freshness. If the road row is ambiguous or the source is stale, it does not invent an OPEN verdict.

How to use this result

Make the decision from the status, freshness and evidence together

Start with the current decision and the trip consequence above it, then check the timestamp and source excerpt. A favorable condition is not a blanket all-clear. If the tool returns VERIFY or LIVE SOURCE UNAVAILABLE, open the official source instead of treating an old reading as current. Recheck close to departure because roads, alerts, water levels and mountain weather can change after you first plan the trip.
