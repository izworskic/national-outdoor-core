    Why this is different

    One waterfall, several independent signals

    The waterfall is first attached to the mapped hydrologic network. From there, the tool looks along that network for relevant observations and infrastructure, retrieves local modeled streamflow and weather forcing, builds a same-season baseline, and only then derives the spectacle forecast.

    USGS NLDIHydrolocation snap, river-network connectivity, gauges and upstream regulation features.
    USGS Water DataLive connected streamflow plus daily history for the seasonal reference.
    NOAA National Water ModelNear-real-time analysis and forecast guidance on the waterfall's local river reach, including ungauged streams.
    National Weather ServiceQuantitative precipitation forecast used as an independent future-flow driver.

    Waterfall Window is an experimental decision-intelligence product. It does not estimate exact curtain width, plume shape, visibility, trail status or safe viewing distance. Modeled flow is guidance rather than an official NWS river forecast. Heavily regulated waterfalls may respond to releases that public national feeds cannot predict precisely.
