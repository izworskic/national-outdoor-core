The decision

What this tool is trying to answer

A West Entrance trip to Old Faithful is a different road decision from North Entrance to Canyon or South Entrance to Old Faithful. The route workbench keeps those segments together instead of collapsing Yellowstone into a misleading park-wide OPEN or CLOSED label.

  • Choose the entrance and destination before interpreting road status.
  • Projected seasonal windows are planning context, not a substitute for the live NPS closure map.
  • Construction and short-term closures can add meaningful delay even when every required road is seasonally available.

How to use this result

Make the decision from the status, freshness and evidence together

Start with the current decision and the trip consequence above it, then check the timestamp and source excerpt. A favorable condition is not a blanket all-clear. If the tool returns VERIFY or LIVE SOURCE UNAVAILABLE, open the official source instead of treating an old reading as current. Recheck close to departure because roads, alerts, water levels and mountain weather can change after you first plan the trip.
